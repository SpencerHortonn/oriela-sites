The program

Real programming, written like a coach. Barbell first, dumbbell and minimal-equipment scaling on everything. Tap a week to see what is inside.

W1

Baseline

Set your numbers

+
  • MonTest day. 1-mile run all out, 10-min AMRAP (5 pull-ups / 10 push-ups / 15 air squats), 3-rep max back squat.
  • TueRun intervals. 6 x 400m at mile pace minus 10 to 15 seconds. 90s rest.
  • WedUpper strength. Bench 4x6, weighted pull-ups 4x5, press and row superset, push-up finisher.
  • ThuTempo run. 20 minutes comfortably hard. Speak in short sentences, not conversations.
  • FriHybrid WOD. "Engine Room": 4 rounds of 400m run, 15 swings, 10 burpees. 25-min cap.
W2

Build

Volume goes up

+
  • MonLower strength. Back squat 5x5, Romanian deadlift 3x8, walking lunges, EMOM finisher.
  • TueRun intervals. 8 x 400m. Hold pace across all eight.
  • WedUpper strength. Bench 5x5, pull-ups 5x5, heavier superset, 10-min AMRAP finisher.
  • ThuLong run. 40 to 50 minutes easy and conversational. Do not race it.
  • FriScored WOD. "Grinder": 800-400-200m runs with deadlifts and pull-ups between. 30-min cap.Scored
W3

Peak

Heaviest week

+
  • MonLower strength. Back squat 4x4 at 80%, front squat, Bulgarian split squats.
  • TueRun intervals. 5 x 800m at hard, sustainable 5K effort. 2:30 rest.
  • WedUpper strength. Bench 4x4, weighted pull-ups, dips and chin-ups, 100 push-ups for time.
  • ThuTempo plus strides. 25 minutes tempo, then 6 x 20-second strides.
  • FriScored WOD. "Prove It": 5 rounds of 400m run, 12 dumbbell thrusters, 12 box jumps. 28-min cap.Scored
W4

Test week

Everything counts

+
  • MonMile retest. All out, same course as week 1. Then light pump work and go home.
  • TueRecovery. Walk, stretch, mobility. That is the whole day.
  • WedAMRAP retest. Same 10-minute test, same standards. Beat your baseline.
  • ThuShakeout. 20 minutes very easy. Visualize tomorrow. Sleep 8 hours.
  • FriSquat retest plus Final Exam. 3RM squat, rest 15, then: 1-mile buy-in plus 3 rounds of 15 pull-ups / 20 push-ups / 25 air squats. 20-min cap.Scored

Pick your division
