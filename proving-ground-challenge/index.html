FAQ

Questions, answered

What equipment do I need?
A barbell or dumbbells, a pull-up bar or something to hang from, and running shoes. Every workout includes dumbbell-only and minimal-equipment scaling, so a basic home gym or commercial gym both work.
I'm a beginner. Can I do this?
Yes. Every session has scaled versions, and the leaderboard has divisions so you compete against athletes near your level. Beginners often improve the most across 4 weeks, which is exactly what the scoring rewards.
How are winners judged?
Your score is a mix of workout performance and consistency across all 28 days. Showing up every day matters as much as going hard. The exact scoring formula and the payout split are published to all athletes before day one.
What if I miss a day?
Life happens. You can make up a missed session within 48 hours and still log a score. Miss more than that and your consistency score takes the hit, which is the point: the challenge rewards people who show up.
What is the refund policy?
Full refund any time before the cohort starts, no questions asked. Once day one begins, entries are locked in because your $49 is already feeding the prize pool.
How much time does it take each day?
Most sessions run 45 to 75 minutes including warmup. Four lifting days, two running days, and one active recovery day per week. It is a real training block, not a side quest.
