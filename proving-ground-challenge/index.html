FILE 01 // PROTOCOL

How it works

Simple on purpose. You train, you log, you get scored. The leaderboard does the rest.

01

Enter

Pick your division, join the cohort. Cohort 01 opens in 2027.

02

Test

Week 1 sets your baseline: a 1-mile run, a 10-minute AMRAP, and a 3-rep max squat. Do not sandbag it. Week 4 measures everything against this.

Train

FILE 04 // BRACKETS

Pick your division

Be honest. Sandbagging a lower division is the fastest way to get booed off the leaderboard. You can move up mid-challenge. You cannot move down.

01
DIVISION 01

Rookie

For lifters in year one. This is where most people belong, and there is no shame in it.

  • Training less than a year
  • Cannot do 5 strict pull-ups yet
  • Improvement scoring favors you
02
DIVISION 02

Contender

The deep end of the middle. Solid base, real numbers, still chasing the top.

  • Training 1 to 3 years
  • Solid base, no elite numbers
  • Biggest field, tightest races
03
DIVISION 03

Elite

Hardest field

You already compete at things. This is the division people watch.

  • 1.5x bodyweight squat, or
  • Sub 6:30 mile, or you compete already
  • Winner takes the biggest cut
FILE 05 // INTEL

Questions

A barbell setup is ideal, but every session has dumbbell and minimal-equipment scaling written in. If you have dumbbells, a pull-up bar, and somewhere to run, you can do the whole challenge.

Yes. That is what the Rookie division is for. Half of the test-week points are for improvement versus your own baseline, so beginners who improve the most can outscore veterans who started fast. That is the point.

You get a 48-hour makeup window and still log full credit. After 48 hours the consistency point is gone for good. Plan your week on Sunday night and this never happens.

370 points total. 20 for consistency (one per training day logged), 150 for the three scored Friday workouts, 200 for test week. Weekly WODs are scored by percentile inside your division. Top 3 overall take the prize pool 50/30/20.

Any score in prize position needs unedited video within 24 hours. No video, no prize, no exceptions. Random audits run all month: fail to produce video within 48 hours and that week's performance scores are wiped. Phone on a tripod is all it takes.

Full refund any time before the cohort starts, no questions asked. Once day one begins, entries lock because your entry is already feeding the prize pool. Injured in week 1? We roll your entry to the next cohort once.
