The ritual

No apps to install. No accounts. Your phone already knows how to do this, the key just gives you a reason to.

01

Set it up once

Thirty seconds in the Shortcuts app, then the key just works. We send a video that walks you through it, but here is the whole thing:

  1. Open Shortcuts, tap Automation, then New Automation.
  2. Choose NFC, tap Scan, and hold the key near the top of your iPhone. Name it "Hearth".
  3. Set it to Run Immediately so there is no pop-up asking permission.
  4. Add the Set Focus action, pick your rest Focus, and set it to Toggle. One tap turns it on, the next tap turns it off.

Works with iPhone Xs and later. No app to install, no account, nothing to charge.

02

Honest by design

The Hearth Key won't stop you from overriding it. Nothing short of a lockbox can. What it does is give the moment a physical anchor: a small, beautiful object that marks the choice to be present. That's what makes it stick.

Questions

Does it work with Android?

The key is an NFC tag, so Android phones can read it, but the one-tap Focus automation is an iPhone Shortcuts feature. Batch one is built for iPhone. Android support is on the roadmap.

What if I lose the key?

Your Focus mode still works manually from Control Center. The key is a shortcut, not the thing itself. And replacements will be available.

Can I really not override it?

You can, and that's the point. The key is a ritual object, not a punishment device. It works because you chose it, not because it forced you.
