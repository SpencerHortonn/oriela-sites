Why this exists

Lantern Club started with a simple rule: phones go in the pouch at the door. What happened next surprised us. People stayed longer. Conversations went deeper. Nobody reached for a screen because there wasn't one to reach for.

We kept getting the same message from people in other cities: "I wish this existed where I live." So we put the night in a box. Same pouch. Same cards. Same candle on the table. Everything we use at our nights, shipped to your door.

How a night works

No planning, no prep. Just do this.

Pouch the phones

Guests arrive. Phones go in the pouch by the door. First thing, no exceptions, no awkwardness.

Light the candle

One flame on the table. That's the signal for the whole room: we're offline now.

Draw a card

Someone draws a card and reads it out loud. Everyone answers. Then draw another.

Talk until you're done

That's it. No agenda, no timer. Most nights run 2 to 3 hours and nobody wants to leave.
