05 Notes

Questions.

05.1How does the free episode work?

Send any episode. We build the full pack: show notes, 8 social posts, newsletter draft, quote graphics. You keep it all, whether or not you sign up. One free episode per show.

05.2Do I need to change how I record?

No. Send the same audio file you already publish. MP3, WAV, or a link to the live episode all work.

05.3Will it sound like me?

Yes. We study your back catalog and match your voice, your vocabulary, and your positions. Growth and Scale plans include a dedicated voice profile for your show.

05.4Who owns the content?

You do, completely. Every word we produce is yours to publish, edit, or ignore.

05.5What if I publish less than my plan covers?

Unused episodes roll over for one month. If you take a season break, pause the plan and pick it back up when you return.

05.6Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly billing, no contracts, no cancellation calls. Quarterly plans exist for shows that want a lower rate, just ask.
