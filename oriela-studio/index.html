Questions

Asked often.

How much does it cost?

Every project is different, so there is no price list. The first call is free, and you get a fixed price in writing before any work starts. If the number does not make sense for you, no hard feelings.

How fast can we start?

Usually within two weeks of the first call. One project at a time gets my full attention, so the calendar is honest about what is free.

Who actually does the work?

Me. You talk to the person filming, editing, and building. Nothing gets handed off to a junior or a subcontractor you have never met.

What if I only need one video, not a retainer?

One-off projects are fine. The retainers exist for people who want content handled every month without thinking about it.
