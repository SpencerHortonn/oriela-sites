FAQ

Questions, answered

How fast is the turnaround?

Podcast episodes are turned around within 5 business days. Short-form video is delivered within a week of the shoot. Websites are quoted with a timeline up front, usually 2 to 4 weeks.

Do I need to be on camera?

For podcast repurposing, never. For short-form video, being on camera helps, but we can build videos around your product, your space, your team, or your customers if you prefer to stay behind the scenes.

Who owns the content?

You do. Everything we make is yours, with full rights to use it anywhere, forever. We may ask to feature finished work in our portfolio.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans are month to month. Cancel before your next billing date and you keep everything we have delivered so far.

How does the free first video work?

We come shoot one short-form video for your business, edit it, and hand it over. No strings, no pitch ambush. If you like it, we talk about a monthly package. If not, the video is still yours.

Do you work outside Tyler, Texas?

Podcast repurposing and websites are fully remote, so anywhere works. Video shoots are in person, so those start in the Tyler area and expand from there.
